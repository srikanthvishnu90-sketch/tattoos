Common questions

How much does Stencil cost?

Waitlist members get free previews at launch. After that, previews cost less than a coffee — and a lot less than a cover-up.

What happens to my photos?

They generate your preview, then they're deleted. Never posted, never sold, never used to train anything.

How accurate is the preview?

It matches your skin tone, lighting, and body contours — built for placement, size, and style decisions. Your artist handles the final line work.
