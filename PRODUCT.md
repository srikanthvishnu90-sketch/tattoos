# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Tattoo-curious first-timers, collectors comparing placement or style, and tattoo artists preparing client consultations. Visitors usually arrive with a tattoo idea but uncertainty about how it will look on a particular body area.

## Product Purpose

Stencil helps people visualize a tattoo on their own body before committing. The marketing page remains the primary surface; a compact try-on experience inside the hero preview demonstrates the essential workflow without displacing the landing-page narrative. Success means a visitor understands the product immediately, can explore a truthful sample, and can securely create a personalized preview when live generation is configured.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, grounded, and quietly confident. Stencil should feel respectful of tattoo craft, transparent about technological limits, and calm during a consequential personal decision.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon cyberpunk tattoo generators, generic AI SaaS dashboards, novelty filters, medical-looking body scanners, over-decorated glass interfaces, and any experience that presents a prepared sample as a personalized result.

## Design Principles

1. Show the outcome early while keeping the workflow understandable.
2. Preserve user control over placement, scale, orientation, and style.
3. Earn trust with explicit consent, honest demo labeling, and clear deletion behavior.
4. Treat AI output as a visualization for an artist conversation, never a guarantee.
5. Keep the tool compact enough that the landing-page story remains primary.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, 44px touch targets, accessible upload controls, non-color-only status indicators, and clear recovery from errors. Use inclusive body-area language and avoid making assumptions about gender, skin tone, age, or anatomy.
